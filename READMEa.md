# 🌿 WILDQUEST AI

> **Turn Screen Time Into Wild Time.**

WILDQUEST AI is an AI-powered outdoor exploration companion designed to encourage people to step away from their screens and reconnect with the real world.

Built for the **DEV/Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge – Week 1: "Touch Grass"**, WILDQUEST AI combines outdoor quests, nature exploration, AI guidance, progress tracking, and privacy-first local AI into one interactive web application.

The goal is simple:

**Use AI to help people spend less time looking at screens and more time exploring the world around them. 🌎🌿**

---

## 🏆 Hackathon Challenge

### Challenge: Touch Grass 🌱

The challenge asks participants to build something using **open-source AI at its core** that encourages people to get away from their screens and engage with the physical world.

Instead of building another application that demands more screen time, WILDQUEST AI uses technology as a bridge between the digital and physical worlds.

### Our Approach

WILDQUEST AI gives users:

- 🌳 Outdoor missions
- 🥾 Walking and exploration quests
- 🐦 Nature observation activities
- 🔎 AI-powered outdoor guidance
- 📍 Privacy-friendly location support
- 🏆 Progress and achievement tracking
- 🤖 Local/open-source AI integration
- 🔒 No mandatory cloud AI API
- 📱 Responsive mobile-friendly interface

The user interacts with the application briefly, receives a mission, and then **puts the phone away to complete it.**

---

# 🌿 What is WILDQUEST AI?

WILDQUEST AI is a digital **AI field companion** for people who want to explore nature, parks, neighborhoods, trails, and outdoor environments.

Instead of asking:

> "What can I do on my phone?"

WILDQUEST AI asks:

> **"What can I discover outside?"**

The application generates small, achievable outdoor challenges based on activities such as:

- Walking
- Bird watching
- Nature observation
- Photography
- Micro-hiking
- Sky observation
- Plant discovery
- Sound hunting
- Urban exploration
- Mindful outdoor activities

# 💡 Problem Statement

Modern technology has made information extremely accessible, but it has also created a major behavioral problem:

**People spend too much time looking at screens.**

Phones, social media, streaming platforms, games, and other digital services are designed to keep users engaged on-screen.

This creates a paradox:

> Technology can connect us to the world while simultaneously disconnecting us from it.

Many people want to:

- Spend more time outside
- Exercise more
- Explore their surroundings
- Learn about nature
- Reduce unnecessary screen time

But they often don't know:

- Where to start
- What to do outside
- What to observe
- How to make outdoor activities interesting
- How to stay motivated

WILDQUEST AI addresses this problem by turning AI into an **outdoor activity generator rather than another screen-time generator.**

# 🎯 Our Solution

WILDQUEST AI creates short, practical outdoor missions.

For example:

### 🐦 Birdwatcher Quest

> Find three different bird calls during a 15-minute walk.

### 🍃 Leaf Detective

> Find three different leaf shapes and compare their edges.

### ☁️ Sky Detective

> Spend 10 minutes observing cloud movement and identify three different cloud formations.

### 🥾 Micro Hike

> Walk for 20 minutes using a route you've never explored before.

### 🔊 Sound Hunt

> Stop for two minutes and identify five different sounds around you.

The application provides the instructions.

**The real-world environment provides the experience.**

# 🤖 Open-Source AI

One of the core principles of WILDQUEST AI is that the AI should not require a proprietary cloud AI service.

The project is designed to work with **locally hosted open-source/open-weight AI models**.

The frontend can communicate with a local AI runtime such as:

```text
Ollama
   ↓
Local Open-Weight Model
   ↓
WILDQUEST AI
   ↓
Outdoor Quest / Guidance
